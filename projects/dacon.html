Restoring Obfuscated Korean Reviews

A national NLP competition on restoring Korean reviews that were deliberately obfuscated at the Jamo (character) level.

Role
Participant
Period
Feb 2025
Tech
Korean PLMs (pko-T5, LLaMA), Python

Overview

Reviews were scrambled at the level of individual Korean letters (Jamo). The task was to recover the original text.

What I did

  • Benchmarked Korean pre-trained language models, including LLaMA and pko-t5-base, reaching an initial accuracy of 0.71.
  • Applied data augmentation and designed a two-stage decoding architecture.
  • Tuned the input representation, decoding flow, and hyperparameters systematically.
  • Validated experimentally how Jamo-level preprocessing affects restoration accuracy.

Result

  • Improved accuracy from 0.71 to 0.74.
