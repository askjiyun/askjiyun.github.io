Restoring Obfuscated Korean Reviews

An NLP competition project focused on restoring Korean reviews distorted by character substitutions, phonetic variations, and nonstandard spelling.

Role
Participant
Period
Feb 2025
Tech
Korean PLMs (pko-T5, LLaMA), Python

Overview

Korean reviews can be obfuscated through character substitutions, phonetic variations, and nonstandard spelling. Restoring the original text requires understanding both Korean linguistic patterns and sentence context.

In the Dacon Korean Review Restoration Competition, I compared Korean pre-trained language models and experimented with data augmentation techniques and training strategies to recover readable, contextually accurate Korean text.

What I did

  • Model Benchmarking & Fine-tuning: Compared Korean pre-trained language models, including LLaMA-DNA, pko-T5, and EXAONE, and experimented with LoRA-based fine-tuning and 4-bit quantization.
  • Korean Text Data Augmentation: Applied character substitutions, phonetic variations, and Korean-specific noise patterns. Combined rule-based augmentation with a reverse obfuscation model, expanding the training dataset from approximately 11,000 to 100,000 samples.
  • Two-Stage Restoration Experiments: Tested a second restoration model to correct errors remaining after the initial inference and compared its performance with a single-stage approach.

Results & Learnings

Experimented with multiple language models and data augmentation strategies, improving the character-level F1 score from approximately 0.71 to 0.74.

The two-stage restoration approach performed worse than single-stage inference, demonstrating that additional inference stages do not necessarily improve text restoration quality.
