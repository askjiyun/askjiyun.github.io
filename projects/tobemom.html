Google Solution Challenge 2024

To Be Mom

A health app that helps pregnant women check their condition and take precautions, built in response to high miscarriage and stillbirth rates worldwide.

Role
Front-end Developer
Period
Dec 2023 – Feb 2024
Tech
Kotlin, Android Studio, Gemini API, Google Maps API

Overview

Many pregnant women cannot visit hospitals often. To Be Mom brings pregnancy management into one app: custom health checklists by pregnancy week, condition checks, a weight and symptom diary, hospital search, and a chatbot.

What I did

  • Developed the Android front end in Kotlin, building the UI screens and key features.
  • Integrated Google's Gemini API to create “Dr. Gemini”, a chatbot that guides users to nearby obstetrics and gynecology clinics via the Google Maps API.
  • Added text-to-speech and speech-to-text so users with low literacy can use the app.

Result

  • Participated in the Google Solution Challenge 2024.
