2nd Place · AI Convergence Department IT Competition

MediMate

An OCR-based mobile app that keeps track of the medicine you have at home and when it expires.

Role
Front-end Developer
Period
Jun – Aug 2024
Tech
React Native, Naver CLOVA OCR

Overview

Medicine at home is easy to forget until it has already expired. MediMate scans drug packaging and prescriptions and keeps an organized list with expiration dates.

What I did

  • Built the front end in React Native: main page, medication scanning, list sorting and search, and other core UI/UX.
  • Connected Naver's CLOVA OCR API to automatically extract drug names, expiration dates, and dosage information from packaging and prescriptions.

Result

  • Won 2nd place in the upperclassmen division of Sungshin Women's University's AI Convergence Department IT Competition.
